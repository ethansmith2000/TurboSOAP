\section{Appendix B. Reproducibility
pointers}\label{appendix-b.-reproducibility-pointers}}

The repository provides the matrix-only \texttt{bf16\_soap.CompiledSOAP}
entry point, frozen campaign manifests/protocols, scalar logs and audit
receipts. The reproducibility guide (\texttt{REPRODUCIBILITY.md})
identifies exact configurations, software, data revisions and hashes,
CPU-only paper regeneration, and the historical launch wrappers. The
claim map (\texttt{CLAIMS.md}) and machine-readable evidence inventory
connect tables and figures to their source artifacts. Experiments save
no trained model/optimizer tensor checkpoints. All reported figures and
numeric tables can be reconstructed from the scalar evidence.

The matched BF16 QR10 evidence (Sections 5.1 and 5.3) is in
\texttt{reports/fastqr\_20261120/}. Stage 1 ran a fresh warm10/QR50
and BF16 QR10 (seed 204, 2,000 calls) sequentially on one exclusive GPU
in the paper's recorded launch environment. Stage 2 continued BF16 QR10
to 10,000 calls on seeds 204 and 205, each run on its own GPU. Every
run reproduced its seed's startup: initial weights, first gradient and
initial CE. The fresh warm arm reproduced the cached main run's
validation CE exactly through call 2000, and the stage-2 seed-204 run
reproduced stage 1 exactly through call 2000. The decision rule for
attributing the endpoint gap was fixed before the stage-2 results. The
FP32-state warm10/QR50 endpoint (3.418128) is the
\texttt{primary\_stock\_warm} arm of the reference's campaign
(\texttt{reports/owt\_restart\_20261026/}), with the same initial
parameters, first gradient, LR and seed as the FP32 QR10 reference.
