\section{Appendix B. Reproducibility
pointers}\label{appendix-b.-reproducibility-pointers}}

The repository provides the matrix-only \texttt{bf16\_soap.CompiledSOAP}
entry point, frozen campaign manifests/protocols, scalar logs and audit
receipts. The reproducibility guide (\texttt{REPRODUCIBILITY.md})
identifies exact configurations, software, data revisions and hashes,
CPU-only paper regeneration, and the historical launch wrappers. The
claim map (\texttt{CLAIMS.md}) and machine-readable evidence inventory
connect tables and figures to their source artifacts. Experiments save
no trained model/optimizer tensor checkpoints. All reported figures and
numeric tables can be reconstructed from the scalar evidence.
