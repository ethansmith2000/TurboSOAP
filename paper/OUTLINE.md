# Paper outline and editorial plan

Working title: **TurboSOAP: Compiled Dense Basis Tracking with BF16 State**.

Central question: how closely can this fixed, dense, compiled BF16-state
implementation follow an audited stock-beta SOAP reference while reducing
state and complete-step cost on one affordable language-modeling workload?

## Proposed structure

1. **Abstract and introduction.** State the approximation objective, two model
   seeds, observed loss gaps, state reduction, and separately scoped timing.
2. **Related work.** Credit original SOAP, subspace/BF16 reparametrization,
   and adaptive basis refinement. Identify the concrete implementation policy
   rather than asserting novelty for warm reuse, BF16, or compilation.
3. **Method.** Give explicit BF16 rounding boundaries; unrounded current moment
   temporaries; warm generator/cap/two corrections; QR reuse and periodic repair;
   M transport and diagonal-V approximation; packing and compilation coverage.
4. **Experimental design.** Specify the model, pinned OWT cohort/tokenizer,
   optimizer grouping and stock matrix betas, initialization calls, full10k
   schedule, validation reuse, reference replay, and timing windows.
5. **Results.** Full learning curves and paired gaps; memory and time; short
   QR20/QR50 controls; isolated QR attribution and numerical observations.
6. **Discussion/limitations.** One workload, two fixed-order initialization
   seeds, original comparator, synthetic attribution, and no equivalence claim.
7. **Conclusion.** Present a measured quality/time/memory operating point.
8. **Appendices.** Development pilot, exact recipes, source/data hashes,
   artifact map and reproduction instructions.

## Claim discipline

Keep the final loss gap and larger early gap visible. The primary reference is
the audited stock-beta QR10 implementation. State approximately50% lower
**matrix state**, not50% lower total training memory. Timing is **less step
time**, with each measurement window named. Preserve all numerical observations
and raw historical screen flags without treating finite crossings as loss vetoes.

The short controls provide early cadence context. The synthetic QR profile
isolates product reuse and records near-tie differences. Neither replaces a
full trained efficient-FP32-QR10 comparator or a matched related-work benchmark.
The earlier pilot is not independent replication of its repeated warm prefix.
